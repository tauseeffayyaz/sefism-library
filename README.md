## Free membership

Sefism is free. There is no subscription, no paywall and no sales call. [Create an account](https://www.sefism.com/auth/signup/) and you get:

**Learn with a plan**

- **[Roadmaps](#roadmaps)**: step-by-step plans in your language or stack, built for AI-assisted learning, with prompts that test what you know.
- **[Cheat sheets](#cheat-sheets)**: the key concepts of a topic on a few pages, with code and common mistakes, made to go with the roadmaps.
- **A saved library**: save the resources you want to your own list.
- **Request a roadmap or cheat sheet**: missing a topic? Ask for it. Others add their vote, and you are told when it is published.

**Get help from a mentor**

- **Get your queries answered**: ask Tauseef anything about your learning, projects or career, and get a written answer in your dashboard and by email.
- **Discuss your project or FYP idea**: share what you are building, or pick any [final year project idea](#final-year-project-ideas) and start a discussion on it. Follow-ups are free, so the conversation can keep going.
- **Free 1:1 sessions**: book a call when talking works better than writing: career direction, choosing between offers, what to learn next, project feedback, LinkedIn. [See the sessions](https://www.sefism.com/mentorship/).
- **Ask for a referral**: browse [companies](#tech-companies-in-pakistan) and request a referral from the company page.

**Show your work**

- **Host your portfolio**: a public portfolio page at `sefism.com/u/your-name` with your about, experience, education, projects, skills and recommendations. Choose which sections are shown.
- **Earn badges**: *Project reviewed* when a mentor reviews your project in writing, *Contributor* when what you share is published on a company page, and recommendations from the Sefism team and Tauseef.

**How credits work**

Credits are a fair-use allowance, not a price. They keep the inbox small enough that every question gets a real answer from a person.

- You start with **10 free credits**.
- A query or a new project discussion costs **1 credit**. Follow-ups in a discussion are free.
- Run out? Request more from the Credits page (5, 10, 20 at a time) and say what they are for. Every request is reviewed and topped up, still for free.
- Roadmaps, cheat sheets, the library and 1:1 sessions never use credits.

## Roadmaps

16 roadmaps in four groups. Roadmap links open after you [sign up](https://www.sefism.com/auth/signup/).

Every roadmap puts the concepts in a clear order, so you always know what to learn next and why it comes before the next thing. They are built for **AI-assisted learning**: you learn with AI, but it does not do the work for you.

- **Your language, framework or stack.** Pick Python or Java, React or Vue, Flutter or Swift, and the stages, resources and code change to match.
- **Stages in order.** Each stage lists the concepts it covers, a milestone that tells you when you are done, and a practical note from Tauseef.
- **AI study prompts that test you.** Paste a stage's prompt into any AI assistant and it quizzes you, from easy to hard, and explains only what you got wrong.
- **Picked resources, not a pile.** Each stage has at most a few: one to learn from (always with a free option), one to practise on, one to keep open as a reference.
- **Checklists and projects.** Doing stages come with concrete checklists, and every roadmap ends in real projects from the FYP library.
- **Your pace, your budget.** Set your hours per week and the plan turns into weeks. Choose free-only and paid picks are hidden. Follow up to six roadmaps at once and tick off stages as you go.

Here is a real stage prompt:

> I am learning Git and the command line. Give me 8 short scenarios one at a time, such as "you committed to main by mistake" or "you want to see which files changed", from easy to hard. Wait for me to type the commands I would run, then tell me what would actually happen and correct only what I got wrong.
