## Income tax on salary, 2026-27

The salaried slabs for July 2026 to June 2027, from the [FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27 (Finance Act, 2026)](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf). The 9% surcharge on salaries over PKR 10 million was withdrawn this year. A guide, not tax advice; [the calculator on the site](https://www.sefism.com/salaries/#take-home) works out your take-home pay.

| Yearly taxable salary | Tax |
| :-- | :-- |
| Up to PKR 600,000 | None |
| PKR 600,000 to PKR 1,200,000 | 1% of the amount over PKR 600,000 |
| PKR 1,200,000 to PKR 2,200,000 | PKR 6,000 + 11% of the amount over PKR 1,200,000 |
| PKR 2,200,000 to PKR 3,200,000 | PKR 116,000 + 20% of the amount over PKR 2,200,000 |
| PKR 3,200,000 to PKR 4,100,000 | PKR 316,000 + 25% of the amount over PKR 3,200,000 |
| PKR 4,100,000 to PKR 5,600,000 | PKR 541,000 + 29% of the amount over PKR 4,100,000 |
| PKR 5,600,000 to PKR 7,000,000 | PKR 976,000 + 32% of the amount over PKR 5,600,000 |
| Over PKR 7,000,000 | PKR 1,424,000 + 35% of the amount over PKR 7,000,000 |
